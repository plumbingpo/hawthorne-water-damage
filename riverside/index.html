Typical Water Damage Problems in Riverside

Ask anyone on our crew what fills the schedule in Riverside and you will hear the same six answers. They are all common, and with every one of them waiting adds to the repair.

Mold: Most mold growth is the follow-up to an earlier leak that seemed to be taken care of. Surfaces dry with towels and a box fan, yet the pad, subfloor and inside of the wall hold moisture. Growth can begin within 24 to 48 hours, then spread quietly until you smell it or see spotting.

Hoses: Every sink, toilet and washer is fed by a flexible connector that holds full house pressure around the clock. A burst connector keeps spraying until somebody closes a valve, and that may take a long while in an empty house. The low-cost defense is to swap out aging hoses and learn where the main shutoff is.

Pumps: Sump pumps, where a home has one, tend to fail at the worst moment after years of running without attention. Stuck float switches, tripped breakers and burned-out motors are what we find most. That leaves the lowest level under a few inches of water, with flooring, wall bases and stored belongings soaked. A battery backup and a quick test before the rainy part of the year go a long way.

Toilets: Among bathroom emergencies in Riverside, the toilet that will not stop rising tops the list. It matters where the water came from, because tank or supply water is clean and used bowl water is not. Either way it moves under tile edges and vanity cabinets, so the floor needs to be checked with a meter.

Cooling: Few people think about the small pipe that carries condensation away from an AC system. If the line plugs or the pan rusts through, water drips into the ceiling or the floor around the unit. Because it happens slowly, the damage in many Riverside homes is well along before a stain appears. Having the line flushed each year, before the cooling season, prevents most of these.

Flooding: When storm drains cannot keep up with a downpour, surface water heads for the lowest doorway it can find. It reaches ground-level rooms, garages and walkout lower levels on the downhill side before anything else. Our Hawthorne Restoration crews extract it, remove what cannot be cleaned and dry the structure to verified readings.
